PERFORMANCE MODEL · V0

Race time equivalent

How it works

The Riegel race-equivalence formula estimates a new finishing time from a known race performance:

T₂ = T₁ × (D₂ / D₁)ⁱ

T is time, D is distance, and i is the fatigue exponent (default 1.06). Results depend on endurance, race conditions, training specificity, and effort. This is a formula-based baseline — not a trained machine-learning model.

Next milestone: train and validate a data-driven model against actual race history, with chronological evaluation and uncertainty calibration.
